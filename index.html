About the course

Responsible no-code AI for task automation.

The GRAPH “Generative AI for Work & Research” course is an 8-week live cohort program focused on practical, responsible use of AI tools for research productivity, automation, analysis, communication, and final project development.

Module 1

Websites, documents & presentations

LLMs, Markdown, LaTeX, HTML, ChatGPT Canvas, Replit, and AI presentation tools.

Module 2

AI-automated data analysis

Code Interpreter, Julius, dashboards, batch file processing, visualizations, and automated reports.

Module 3

Research & text analysis

Literature reviews, text classification, sentiment analysis, knowledge maps, and summarization workflows.

Module 4

Custom GPTs and chatbots

RAG systems, custom GPTs, Botpress workflows, and research knowledge bases.

Module 5

AI for task automation

Agents, email response workflows, browser control agents, and app-to-app automation.

Module 6

Communication and outreach

Research summaries, scientific figures, AI video abstracts, visualization prompts, and content scheduling.

Module 7

Capstone project & showcase

Peer feedback, project refinement, final presentations, and public Q&A.

Assessment

Quizzes, assignments and final project

Course grading combines quizzes, workshop assignments, attendance, participation, and a final project.
